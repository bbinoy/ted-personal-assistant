For each email return a label:
act_now    - needs {{who}}'s own input, decision or action soon: a person waiting on them, a deadline, something only they can answer or approve.
handle     - an assistant can deal with it without {{who}}: e.g. a meeting-scheduling email that can be answered from their calendar (a person asking for a time, offering one, or confirming a time the owner suggested: the assistant checks the calendar and drafts the reply), or a booking/confirmation that needs a calendar event.

archive    - safe to archive immediately: newsletters, automated notifications, marketing, cold outreach, system messages.
Judge by intent, not by sender or format: ask what is left to do. If the email only records or reports something that already happened or that a system already applied, then nothing is left for {{who}} or for an assistant, so the label is archive (kind notification). That holds even when the news is unwelcome, because the system behind it also tells them directly. "handle" means there is still work left that an assistant can finish; "read_later" means something from a person or source {{who}} would want to read themselves. Anything with an open question, a payment or deadline still ahead, an official or legal letter that may need a response, or a person waiting stays in the inbox.
Examples of the idea, not a list: a card receipt, a calendar invitation update or reply, and a delivery or travel status notice are archive. An invoice still to be paid, an insurer's notice of a policy change, and a colleague's question are not.
